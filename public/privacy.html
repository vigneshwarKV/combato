Privacy Policy — Combato

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Combato ("the app", "we") is a game where players write strategies for AI fighters and compete on a leaderboard. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Information we collect

  • Account identity. When you sign in with Google we receive your Google account's basic profile: your name, email address, and a unique account ID. When you "Play as Guest" we create an anonymous account with a random ID only — no email or name.
  • Display handle. The fighter name you choose (or your Google display name), shown on the leaderboard.
  • Gameplay data. Match results used for scoring: mode, win/loss, HP remaining, whether it was a KO, match duration, points, and timestamps.

We do not collect location, contacts, device identifiers for advertising, or payment information. The app contains no ads.

How we use it

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in across sessions.
  • To compute and display the daily and weekly leaderboards.
  • To protect the service from abuse (e.g. rate limiting).

Where it is stored

Account and gameplay data are stored using Supabase (our database and authentication provider). Authentication via Google is handled by Google Sign-In. These providers process data on our behalf.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. Your display handle and score are visible to other players on the public leaderboard. Data is shared only with the service providers above as needed to run the app.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account and match history while your account exists. To delete your account and associated data, contact us at vig422025@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Children

Combato is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
